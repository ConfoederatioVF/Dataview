## UI.

AVOID AI SLOP.

1. Reserve monospace for code.
2. Restrict your colour palette and visual composition.
3. Do not write more than you need to: prioritise brevity and precision.
4. Glassmorphism over gradients; do not use sparkles in icons.

COLOURS AND THEMING.

- Primary Font: Karla, 0.85-1rem
- Monospace Font (code/keybinds only): Fira Code, 0.85-1rem
- White (255,255,255): primary colour
- Red (200,40,40): interactive elements; toggled off
- Green (60,150,60): toggled on

For icons, use Material Icons.

1. Layouts should be simple and organised. Do not lose feature depth.
